Data Deletion

This page covers Keycloak, Google, and GitHub sign-in. The staging provider endpoint also advertises Facebook; Facebook is not enabled in production. Morda Labs is the project name, not a separately registered legal entity. The hosted demo has no separate application database dedicated to normalized user profiles, but Keycloak has persistent account data in PostgreSQL.

Request deletion

Email contact@mordalabs.com to request deletion of information associated with your demo use. Tell us which sign-in provider and account the request concerns; do not send passwords or tokens. Requests are reviewed and handled manually. Individual Keycloak accounts can be deleted administratively, but the publicly documented Keycloak demo account is shared and does not identify a private visitor account.

Signing out ends your demo session; it does not automatically delete provider accounts, Keycloak account data, technical logs, backups, or all other stored information. OVH Automated Backup displays a seven-day restoration window, but its exact scope and database recovery consistency are unverified, so we cannot promise an exact removal time for backup copies. See the Privacy Policy for more about logs and storage.

Provider accounts

Google, GitHub, and Facebook control information in their own accounts. Contact the provider or use its account controls for data it holds. Removing a provider's authorization for this demo does not by itself delete information held by Keycloak or in demo logs and backups.

Facebook-specific guidance

If Facebook shows the demo in your app settings, you can remove its access there and use Facebook's own data controls for information held by Facebook. You can also email us about information associated with your demo interaction. Facebook sign-in is not enabled on production.

Questions or concerns

For questions about a deletion request, email contact@mordalabs.com. To report a security issue, email security@mordalabs.com.
