Who it is for

Teams building browser-facing .NET products.

OIDC Starter Premium is for software houses, product consultancies, and product teams using or adopting OAuth/OIDC in ASP.NET Core BFF applications.

What your team would otherwise design, test, and maintain across BFF projects

Capable teams can build these pieces. Premium's value is a reusable implementation of their combined behavior, with ongoing maintenance instead of repeating the design and regression work for each project.

The host still owns deployment, identity-provider configuration, and application authorization.
